Project 8

Research article

LLM Output Quality Study

Co-authored a research article that compares the output quality of ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek through users’ eyes. 99 respondents rated responses on relevance, accuracy, completeness, clarity, and consistency, once without knowing which model wrote them (blind) and once knowing it (non-blind).

Statistical analysis with the Friedman test found no significant difference between the models in either setting, yet ChatGPT gained preference when its name was shown. This suggests that knowing a model’s identity can influence what people prefer.

Authors: Jesslyn Jennifer Wibowo, Verren Angelica Hersan, Mario Javier Christianus, Rimeylinda Ariela Tjokrowijaya, and Dr. RA Dyah Wahyu Sukmaningsih. Information Systems Study Program, BINUS University.

Competition 4

Digital Awareness Campaign Poster

Created a digital safety awareness campaign focused on preventing online scams. The concept, “Chameleon in Disguise, Keep Your Data Safe,” uses a chameleon as a visual metaphor for online scammers who can disguise themselves as trusted individuals or organizations. The poster highlights three practical precautions: avoiding suspicious links, never sharing OTPs or personal information, and verifying information before trusting it.

The design combines bold typography, contrasting colors, and simple visual icons to deliver the message in a clear and accessible way. It encourages audiences to stay alert and protect their personal data when interacting online.
